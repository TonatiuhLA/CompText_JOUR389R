+ GIVAUDAN S.A. LAUNCHES SCENTAURUS M13: A BREAKTHROUGH IN PET ODOR CONTROL
Givaudan S.A. has unveiled its latest innovation, the Scentaurus M13, designed to revolutionize pet odor control through advanced AI-driven fragrance detection. This sophisticated device, which hit the market recently, aims to offer both industrial and home-care formulations a new level of precision in malodor management. On 27th June 2026, Givaudan's shares (ISIN CH0010645932) saw a notable uptick as investors reacted positively to the announcement.
The Scentaurus M13 operates as an analytical instrument that quantifies malodor molecules, particularly those linked to pet and household odors. By employing real-time data from its proprietary sensors, the M13 provides formulators with insights that were previously reliant on human perception. This development is set to shorten the cycle for creating more effective cleaning and deodorizing products, enhancing Givaudan's position in the competitive home-care market.
According to Dr. Marie Dupont, the product manager, the Scentaurus platform acts like a 'trained nose with a repeat button', integrating advanced pattern-recognition algorithms with Givaudan's extensive fragrance library. This allows the M13 to detect subtle changes in malodor intensity, which human panels might miss, particularly for volatile sulfur compounds responsible for unpleasant pet odors. The data generated can be directly applied to internal performance metrics, streamlining the development process for manufacturers.
Givaudan positions this innovative technology as part of a broader initiative to enhance its pet-care product line. The sensitivity of the M13 to sulfur compounds enables brands to not only mask but genuinely neutralize common pet odors, supported by quantifiable lab results. CEO Gilles Andrier has emphasized that this data-driven approach will differentiate Givaudan from its competitors, especially as retailers increasingly demand verification for product claims.
While the Scentaurus M13 offers significant advantages in odor analysis, it is not without limitations. It complements, rather than replaces, traditional sensory panels and more complex analytical tools. Nonetheless, Givaudan's strategic move to integrate Scentaurus M13 into its operations is expected to solidify long-term partnerships with clients in the pet-care segment, fostering growth in a market that is increasingly competitive.
+ CREATIVE BIOLABS ENHANCES PHAGE LIBRARY PLATFORMS FOR BIOTHERAPEUTIC ADVANCEMENTS
SHIRLEY, NY, April 30, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/-- As demand grows for faster gene discovery and more efficient biomolecule engineering, phage technologies have become indispensable tools across modern life sciences. Creative Biolabs' newly upgraded lambda and M13 platforms offer complementary strengths that streamline the entire workflow—from genome scale exploration to high throughput functional screening.
The high-fidelity lambda phage genome libraries remain a preferred system for constructing genomic libraries due to their ability to accommodate large DNA inserts of up to 25 kb while maintaining stable genomic representation. Creative Biolabs has modernized this classical platform with advanced technologies that significantly enhance library fidelity, coverage, and long-term stability. Key upgrades include acoustic DNA shearing for highly controlled and reproducible fragmentation, optimized size-selection workflows ensuring ideal insert length distribution, and ultra-efficient in vitro packaging extracts that achieve titers exceeding 10 pfu/ g DNA.
In addition, Creative Biolabs has implemented bias-minimized protocols tailored for complex or non-model organisms. These innovations enable researchers to capture rare genomic elements and construct libraries with exceptional complexity, supporting applications such as whole-genome mapping, metagenomics, gene discovery, and functional screening.
Meanwhile, Creative Biolabs' upgraded M13 phage display platform supports the construction of ultra-diverse libraries—often reaching 10 to 10 unique variants—ideal for identifying high-affinity antibodies, peptides, and other ligands. The platform highlights include multiple display formats, including pIII monovalent and pVIII multivalent configurations, as well as Trimer Codon Technology to eliminate stop codons and ensure uniform amino acid distribution.
These capabilities make the platform a powerful engine for antibody discovery, epitope mapping, protein engineering, and diagnostic development. A spokesperson for Creative Biolabs stated, "Phage based technologies continue to shape the future of biotechnology. Our upgraded lambda and M13 platforms reflect our commitment to delivering advanced, customizable, and reliable solutions that help researchers achieve meaningful scientific breakthroughs."

+ GENERAL CATALYST AND INDIA QUOTIENT BACK OMLI WITH $1.5 MILLION FOR AI IN CHILDREN'S DEVELOPMENT
Gurugram-based Omli, focused on developing artificial intelligence (AI) infrastructure for children's speech and language development, has raised $1.5 million in a seed funding round led by India Quotient, with participation from General Catalyst. The funding announcement was made on 17th June 2026.
Founded in 2025 by Shivam Munshi and Siddhant Garg, Omli aims to fill a notable gap in the AI landscape by creating speech models tailored specifically for young children. Most existing commercial speech recognition systems are predominantly trained on adult voices, leading to inaccuracies when interacting with children, whose speech patterns and tones differ significantly from those of adults.
Omli's flagship application, OmliKids, enables children aged three to ten years to engage in voice-based conversations with an AI companion named Doro. Distinct from other children's digital products that heavily rely on games or video content, Omli is positioning its app as a conversational platform designed to enhance speaking skills and logical thinking. The app has already achieved over 500,000 downloads on the Google Play Store within a few months of its launch.
To address the challenges associated with conventional speech models, Omli has developed VedAnsh-v1, a proprietary speech-to-text and voice activity detection system specifically designed for children's voices. This innovative model has shown significant improvements in word error rates compared to existing commercial systems when tested with children's speech. Co-founder Siddhant Garg emphasized the company's ambition to achieve adult-grade accuracy in children's speech recognition, including support for Indian accents and vernacular languages.
With a current team of around 15 employees, Omli is in the process of developing the next generation of its speech model and aims to expand into conversational intelligence systems that can adapt in real-time to a child's speaking pace and communication patterns. The startup also plans to create speech-based screening tools to help parents and educators identify articulation challenges early on. Shivam Munshi noted that today's children are often seen silently scrolling on screens, underscoring the need for technology that encourages verbal interaction. Investors see promise in this under-served market, with India Quotient's Madhukar stating that children represent the largest consumer segment for which AI solutions are yet to be developed.
+ GENERAL CATALYST LEADS $85M GROWTH ROUND FOR RYLO, BOOSTING TOTAL FUNDING BEYOND $100M
Rylo, formerly known as Nagish, has secured $85 million in growth funding in a round led by General Catalyst’s Customer Value Fund, with participation from Canaan, Vertex Ventures, and Contour. This latest investment pushes the company’s total funding beyond $100 million and reflects growing investor confidence in AI-driven accessibility technologies.
Based in Tel Aviv and New York, Rylo focuses on developing communication solutions that improve accessibility for Deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals. The company offers an AI-powered communication platform designed to make everyday interactions more accessible. Its technology provides real-time captioning for phone calls, instant speech-to-text conversion, text-to-speech capabilities, and in-person transcription services. These tools help users communicate more effectively in personal, professional, and public environments while reducing reliance on traditional interpretation services.
Rylo has expanded its product ecosystem through innovation and strategic acquisitions. Following its acquisition of Sign.mt in 2025, the company launched Rylo Sign, a solution that enables real-time translation between spoken and sign languages. The platform also incorporates advanced AI features such as speaker recognition, sentiment analysis, and fraud detection, enhancing both usability and communication accuracy across multiple channels.
Investors view Rylo as a strong player within the rapidly growing accessibility technology sector. The company addresses a long-standing market need by leveraging artificial intelligence to deliver scalable, cost-effective communication solutions. As accessibility regulations evolve and digital communication becomes increasingly important, demand for technologies that remove communication barriers continues to rise worldwide.
The newly raised capital will support Rylo’s plans to expand its engineering, product development, and commercial operations teams. The company also intends to invest in AI research, infrastructure development, and new workplace accessibility solutions. By continuously refining its AI models and expanding its suite of communication tools, Rylo aims to improve reliability, accuracy, privacy, and user independence. Looking ahead, Rylo is pursuing a long-term strategy focused on global expansion and broader adoption among both individual and enterprise users.

+ LIGHTSPEED VENTURE PARTNERS DIVESTS $15.1 MILLION IN NETSKOPE SHARES
MENLO PARK – Lightspeed Opportunity Fund, L.P., an entity associated with Lightspeed Venture Partners, reported the sale of Netskope Inc. (NASDAQ:NTSK) Class A common stock totaling approximately $15.1 million, according to a recent SEC filing. The date of these transactions was 12th June 2026 and 15th June 2026, as Netskope shares traded at $8.95, down roughly 60% over the past year and near its 52-week low of $7.67.
On 12th June 2026, Lightspeed Opportunity Fund, L.P. converted 1,650,000 shares of Class B common stock into Class A common stock for no additional consideration. Following this conversion, the fund sold 1,313,827 shares of Netskope Class A common stock at a weighted average price of $9.19 per share. Two days later, on 15th June 2026, Lightspeed Opportunity Fund, L.P. sold an additional 336,173 shares of Class A common stock at a weighted average price of $9.00 per share. The total value of these sales amounted to $15,099,627, with the weighted average prices for the transactions ranging between $9.00 and $9.19. Following these sales, Lightspeed Opportunity Fund, L.P. holds no Class A common stock.
While Lightspeed Opportunity Fund, L.P. has divested its Class A common stock, several other Lightspeed entities continue to hold significant amounts of Netskope Class B common stock. These Class B shares are convertible into Class A common stock on a one-for-one basis at the option of the holder or automatically on 19th September 2035. As of 12th June 2026, these and other related Lightspeed entities collectively held 62,843,207 shares of Class B common stock, representing potential Class A shares.
In other recent developments, Netskope reported annual recurring revenue (ARR) of $845 million, reflecting a 29% year-over-year increase, which met general market expectations but fell short of some analysts’ estimates. The company’s total revenue reached approximately $202 million, marking a 28% year-over-year growth and slightly surpassing market forecasts. Despite these figures, several financial firms have adjusted their price targets for Netskope, with TD Cowen reducing its target from $25 to $19 while maintaining a Buy rating, citing the ARR miss and the exit of the CFO as influencing factors.
BMO Capital and RBC Capital both lowered their targets to $13, maintaining an Outperform rating, with concerns about the company’s ARR growth and market competition. Piper Sandler decreased its target to $18, noting strong new logo ARR growth and traction with new AI products but highlighting challenges with sales force productivity. Mizuho also cut its target to $13, acknowledging the modest revenue beat but pointing out it was the smallest upside since Netskope became a public company. These developments illustrate the varied analyst perspectives on Netskope’s recent performance.
