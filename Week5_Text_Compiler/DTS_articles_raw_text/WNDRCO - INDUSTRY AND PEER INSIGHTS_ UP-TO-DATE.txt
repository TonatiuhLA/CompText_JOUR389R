WndrCo, founded in 2017 by entrepreneur Jeffrey Katzenberg and former Disney executive David Ellison, is a Los Angeles-based holding company dedicated to acquiring control stakes in underappreciated technology firms and investing in innovative startups to accelerate their growth into market leaders. Its core business activities encompass strategic investments, operational enhancements, and scaling support across consumer and enterprise technology sectors, with a focus on lead institutional funding in post-product-market-fit rounds and seed-stage opportunities for next-generation entrepreneurs. Notable portfolio companies include Aura, offering comprehensive identity and financial protection services; Writer, an enterprise AI writing assistant; and Yassir, a leading ride-hailing and delivery platform in the Middle East and Africa. Among its fastest-growing segments, online security solutions-exemplified by Aura and Point Wild''s portfolio of protective brands-stand out for surging demand amid rising cyber threats, while enterprise AI tools like Writer are rapidly expanding, capitalizing on the global shift toward generative technologies and automation. Through these initiatives, WndrCo drives transformative value, fostering innovation and market dominance in high-potential domains.

+   LATAM MICRODRAMA STARTUP IDILIO RAISES $5.5 MILLION SEED WITH WNDRCO INVESTMENT
WndrCo backed a seed round worth 5.5 million for Idilio, a platform focused on microdrama for Latin America, with the news released on 4th June 2026. The investment highlights a trend toward technology enabled storytelling in regional markets. Idilio leverages an AI engine to streamline production while preserving cultural relevance. 
Idilio was founded by Gabriela Tafur and Esteban Ramirez, combining the appeal of telenovelas with the fast pace of vertical video to reach mobile audiences across the region. 
Within five months of launch, Idilio reports 1.5 million downloads across 93 countries and 40 million episodes streamed. Average daily viewing runs around 50 minutes, signaling strong engagement in a market where localized content matters. 
The company also introduced Idilio Creators, a program that allows creators to monetize content directly on the platform, with profits fully returned to creators. Investors in the round include a16z Speedrun, Goodwater Capital, Precursor Ventures, and David Velez, alongside WndrCo.

+   STRATEGIC PRIVATIZATION OF JANUS HENDERSON: TRIAN, GENERAL CATALYST AND QIA BACK THE MOVE
Janus Henderson Group Ltd. has completed its take-private transaction with Trian Fund Management, General Catalyst Group Management, and the Qatar Investment Authority. The deal, finalized on the 30th of June, 2026, converts public shares into cash at $52.00 and removes the company from the NYSE.
The move shifts Janus Henderson toward a longer-term ownership model, directing capital to client-centric investments in investment platforms, technology, and talent development. CEO Ali Dibadj will continue to lead the business from its London and Denver offices.
The privatization is supported by a group of global investors including MassMutual and Sun Hung Kai & Co., with financial and legal advisers guiding the process. The company’s leadership says private ownership will strengthen its competitive position in asset management.
+   OMLI RAISES $1.5 MILLION SEED TO BUILD AI FOR KIDS' SPEECH AND LANGUAGE
Gurugram startup Omli has announced a $1.5 million seed round led by India Quotient with participation from General Catalyst, disclosed on June 17, 2026.
Omli focuses on building AI infrastructure for children's speech and language development, addressing a gap where most commercial speech models are trained on adult voices.
Its OmliKids app lets kids aged three to ten talk with an AI companion named Doro, aiming to be a conversational platform that helps improve speaking skills and logical thinking; the app has attracted more than 500,000 downloads on Google Play in a few months.
To tackle the limitations of existing models, Omli developed VedAnsh-v1, a child-specific speech-to-text and voice activity detection system that showed improvements in word error rates when tested with children's speech. With about 15 employees, Omli plans to roll out the next generation of the model and explore real-time conversational intelligence and early articulation screening tools.
+   AI-DRIVEN ACCESSIBILITY STARTUP RYLO RAISES $85M GROWTH ROUND BACKED BY GENERAL CATALYST AND PARTNERS
Rylo has secured $85 million in growth funding in a round led by General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund, with participation from Canaan, Vertex Ventures, and Contour. This investment lifts total funding to beyond $100 million and underscores investor confidence in AI-driven accessibility technology.
The company, headquartered in Tel Aviv and New York, builds an AI-powered platform that improves accessibility for Deaf and hard-of-hearing users. Its tools include real-time captioning for phone calls, instant speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and in-person transcription, designed to support everyday communication across home, work, and public settings.
Rylo has grown its ecosystem through innovation and strategic moves, most notably the 2025 acquisition of Sign.mt and the launch of Rylo Sign, which enables real-time translation between spoken and sign languages. Additional AI features such as speaker recognition, sentiment analysis, and fraud detection enhance performance across channels.
The investment signals a positive outlook for the accessibility technology sector as demand increases with broader digital communication and evolving regulations. The capital will fund hiring in engineering, product, and commercial teams, plus AI research and infrastructure, with a long-term plan for global expansion and broader adoption by individuals and organizations.

Switch is pursuing a private round near $2B with Andreessen Horowitz leading, announced on 3 July 2026. Valuation and product specifics remain undisclosed, but the backing by A16Z signals confidence in Switch's long-term strategy.

+   PILLAR RAISES $20 MILLION SEED ROUND WITH A16Z AT THE HELM, AIMING TO MODERNIZE RISK MANAGEMENT
Pillar has raised $20 million in a seed round led by Andreessen Horowitz, with participation from Dara Khosrowshahi and other investors.
The round underscores investor confidence in Pillar’s mission to simplify financial risk management as firms contend with volatility, currency risk, and evolving regulations.
Pillar aims to move teams away from spreadsheet- and legacy-based workflows toward an integrated platform for treasury oversight, hedging, and regulatory compliance as companies expand globally.
The funds will fuel product development and drive early customer adoption as Pillar positions itself in a space viewed as essential to modern enterprise operations.
+   ANDREESSEN HOROWITZ BACKS PROSPER AI, FUELING GROWTH IN HEALTHCARE ADMINISTRATIVE AUTOMATION
Prosper AI has raised $30 million in a Series A led by Andreessen Horowitz to scale its end-to-end patient-management platform.
The company reports revenue growth of five times and that its platform now manages more than $1.3 billion in patient care as of 30th June 2026.
Prosper AI automates scheduling, insurance verification, patient billing and payer communications to reduce administrative costs by over 40% and streamline the patient journey from appointment requests to reimbursement.
Client names include Athenahealth and Jackson Memorial Hospital; the new funds will expand engineering, boost customer engagement and deepen EHR integrations.
