M13 General Partner Latif Peracha announced on April 15, 2026, that the early-stage venture capital firm has agreed to co-lead OpenFX's Series A funding round. Peracha stated that M13's involvement is driven by a shared vision of enhancing the trading experience for both retail and institutional investors. OpenFX is a digital asset platform focused on transforming the foreign exchange market, addressing persistent industry challenges such as high fees and slow transaction times. The funding round is designed to strengthen OpenFX's technological infrastructure and expand its market footprint, positioning it as a competitive force within the evolving digital finance landscape. Peracha noted that M13's experience in scaling technology companies will provide critical operational guidance as OpenFX executes on its growth plans, including the rollout of new product features targeting its expanding user base.
Separately, Givaudan S.A. unveiled the Scentaurus M13 on June 27, 2026, a new analytical instrument designed to quantify malodor molecules, particularly those associated with pet and household odors. The device employs real-time data from proprietary sensors to give product formulators insights that were previously dependent on human sensory panels. Givaudan's shares (ISIN CH0010645932) moved higher on the day of the announcement as investors responded positively to the launch. Product manager Dr. Marie Dupont described the Scentaurus platform as functioning like a "trained nose with a repeat button," integrating pattern-recognition algorithms with Givaudan's fragrance library to detect volatile sulfur compounds that human panels often miss. CEO Gilles Andrier emphasized that this data-driven methodology will differentiate Givaudan from competitors as retailers increasingly require verifiable performance data to support product claims. The Scentaurus M13 is intended to complement, rather than replace, traditional sensory evaluation panels and is positioned as a strategic tool for deepening client partnerships in the pet-care segment.
On a parallel track within the life sciences space, Creative Biolabs announced on April 30, 2026, the enhancement of its lambda and M13 phage library platforms to meet growing demand for faster gene discovery and more efficient biomolecule engineering. The upgraded lambda phage genome libraries now feature acoustic DNA shearing for precise and reproducible fragmentation, optimized size-selection workflows, and ultra-efficient in vitro packaging extracts achieving titers exceeding 10 pfu/g DNA. These improvements allow researchers to capture rare genomic elements and build libraries suited to whole-genome mapping, metagenomics, and functional screening. The upgraded M13 phage display platform supports the construction of ultra-diverse libraries reaching up to 10 to the 10th power unique variants, enabling high-affinity antibody discovery, epitope mapping, protein engineering, and diagnostic development. A spokesperson for Creative Biolabs stated, "Phage-based technologies continue to shape the future of biotechnology. Our upgraded lambda and M13 platforms reflect our commitment to delivering advanced, customizable, and reliable solutions that help researchers achieve meaningful scientific breakthroughs."
Turning to peer activity, General Catalyst completed a landmark transaction on June 30, 2026, as Janus Henderson Group Ltd. finalized its take-private deal involving Trian Fund Management, General Catalyst Group Management, and Qatar Investment Authority. Janus Henderson's public shares were converted into cash at $52.00 per share and subsequently delisted from the New York Stock Exchange. The firm will continue to operate under the leadership of CEO Ali Dibadj, maintaining its headquarters in London and Denver. Dibadj described the transition as a transformative opportunity to invest in investment solutions, technology, and talent development over a longer time horizon. General Catalyst CEO Hemant Taneja highlighted the partnership's focus on enhancing Janus Henderson's technological capabilities and broader growth strategy. The transaction was supported by notable global investors including MassMutual and Sun Hung Kai & Co., with legal and financial advisory services provided by leading industry firms.
General Catalyst also participated in a $1.5 million seed funding round for Gurugram-based Omli, announced on June 17, 2026, with India Quotient leading the round. Founded in 2025 by Shivam Munshi and Siddhant Garg, Omli develops artificial intelligence infrastructure tailored for children's speech and language development, addressing a gap in existing AI systems that are predominantly trained on adult voices. The company's flagship application, OmliKids, enables children aged three to ten to engage in voice-based conversations with an AI companion named Doro, and had surpassed 500,000 downloads on the Google Play Store within months of its launch. Omli has developed VedAnsh-v1, a proprietary speech-to-text and voice activity detection model specifically calibrated for children's speech patterns, showing measurable improvements in word error rates compared to existing commercial systems. Co-founder Siddhant Garg indicated the company's ambition to achieve adult-grade accuracy in children's speech recognition, including support for Indian accents and vernacular languages. India Quotient's Madhukar pointed to children as the largest consumer segment for which AI solutions are yet to be meaningfully developed.
General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund led an $85 million growth funding round for Rylo, formerly known as Nagish, with participation from Canaan, Vertex Ventures, and Contour. The round pushed Rylo's total funding beyond $100 million. Headquartered across Tel Aviv and New York, Rylo develops AI-powered communication solutions for Deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals, offering real-time call captioning, speech-to-text conversion, text-to-speech functionality, and in-person transcription services. Following its acquisition of Sign.mt in 2025, the company launched Rylo Sign, enabling real-time translation between spoken and sign languages. The platform also incorporates speaker recognition, sentiment analysis, and fraud detection capabilities. The newly raised capital is earmarked for expanding Rylo's engineering, product development, and commercial operations teams, alongside investment in AI research, infrastructure development, and new workplace accessibility solutions.
Lightspeed Venture Partners disclosed the sale of approximately $15.1 million in Netskope Inc. (NASDAQ: NTSK) Class A common stock through its Lightspeed Opportunity Fund, L.P., with transactions recorded on June 12 and June 15, 2026. On June 12, the fund converted 1,650,000 Class B shares into Class A shares and subsequently sold 1,313,827 Class A shares at a weighted average price of $9.19 per share. On June 15, an additional 336,173 Class A shares were sold at a weighted average price of $9.00 per share, bringing total proceeds to $15,099,627. Following these transactions, Lightspeed Opportunity Fund, L.P. holds no remaining Class A common stock in Netskope, though other affiliated Lightspeed entities collectively retained 62,843,207 Class B shares as of June 12, 2026. Netskope reported annual recurring revenue of $845 million, representing 29% year-over-year growth, and total revenue of approximately $202 million, up 28% year-over-year. Multiple analysts revised their price targets downward in response, with TD Cowen lowering its target from $25 to $19, BMO Capital and RBC Capital setting targets at $13, and Mizuho and Piper Sandler also adjusting their outlooks, citing ARR performance and CFO departure as contributing factors.
Lightspeed Venture Partners also moved to strengthen its marketing leadership with the appointment of Rohit Kaul as its new marketing head, a development that coincides with the firm's preparations to raise its fifth India and Southeast Asia-focused fund, reportedly targeting approximately $500 million. Kaul previously served as Vice President of Marketing at Blume Ventures from February 2023, with additional experience at Sacra and HCL. His appointment follows the departure of former VP of Marketing and Communications Mansi Chugh after nearly four years in the role. Lightspeed has been among the most active investors in India's startup ecosystem, with portfolio companies including OYO, Udaan, ShareChat, PhysicsWallah, and Pocket FM. The firm's investment in marketing leadership signals a deliberate effort to reinforce its brand presence as it navigates a period of slowing startup funding and heightened performance scrutiny across its regional portfolio.
Rounding out recent Lightspeed activity, the firm participated in back-to-back funding rounds for an agent evaluation startup, with the investment publicly acknowledged on May 13, 2026. The startup has developed a platform designed to streamline agent performance assessments, attracting consecutive support from Lightspeed as the firm continues to identify niche technology sectors with significant operational impact. The capital is being directed toward expanding the startup's product capabilities and scaling its go-to-market strategy as demand for AI-driven evaluation tools grows across multiple industries.
