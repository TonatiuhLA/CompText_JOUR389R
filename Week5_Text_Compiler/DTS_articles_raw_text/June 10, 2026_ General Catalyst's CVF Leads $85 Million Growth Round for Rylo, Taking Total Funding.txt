Rylo has secured $85 million in a growth round led by General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund, with participation from Canaan, Vertex Ventures, and Contour. The investment pushes the company's total funding beyond $100 million and reflects growing investor confidence in AI-driven accessibility technologies.
Based in Tel Aviv and New York, Rylo develops AI-powered communication tools to improve accessibility for Deaf and hard-of-hearing people. The platform provides real-time captioning for phone calls, instant speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and in-person transcription, helping users communicate more effectively in personal, professional, and public environments while reducing reliance on traditional interpretation services.
Rylo has expanded its product ecosystem through innovation and acquisitions, including the 2025 purchase of Sign.mt. The company launched Rylo Sign, enabling real-time translation between spoken and sign languages, and has integrated advanced AI features such as speaker recognition, sentiment analysis, and fraud detection across multiple channels. The newly raised capital will support expanding its engineering, product development, and commercial operations, as well as AI research and infrastructure, with a long-term strategy focused on global expansion and broader adoption among both individuals and enterprise users.

1.1 ACTIVITIES
General Catalyst, established in 2000 and headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts, operates as a leading global investment and transformation firm dedicated to partnering with visionary entrepreneurs. Its core business activities revolve around providing venture capital across all stages-from seed funding to growth equity-while offering strategic advisory services, operational expertise, and ecosystem integration to cultivate resilient enterprises. The firm invests in a broad spectrum of sectors, including healthcare, financial technology, defense, industrials, climate, and energy, with a strong emphasis on fostering innovation that yields both financial returns and societal benefits. Among its fastest-growing segments, applied artificial intelligence emerges prominently, fueled by a landmark $8 billion capital commitment in October 2024 to accelerate AI-centric startups and transformations. Managing over $30 billion in assets, General Catalyst''s portfolio includes transformative companies such as Airbnb and Stripe, underscoring its pivotal role in scaling high-impact ventures worldwide.
