Rylo has closed an $85 million growth round led by General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund, with participation from Canaan, Vertex Ventures, and Contour. The financing brings Rylo's total funding to over $100 million, reflecting strong confidence in AI-driven accessibility technologies.
Headquartered in Tel Aviv and New York, Rylo is building an AI-powered platform that makes communication more accessible for Deaf and hard-of-hearing users. Its offerings include real-time captioning for phone calls, instant speech-to-text conversion, text-to-speech, and in-person transcription, helping users engage more fully in personal, professional, and public settings.
Rylo has expanded its product line through innovation and acquisitions, notably purchasing Sign.mt in 2025 and launching Rylo Sign for real-time spoken-to-sign translation. The platform now also features speaker recognition, sentiment analysis, and fraud detection to improve usability and accuracy across channels.
The new capital will fund growth across engineering, product development, and commercial operations, as well as ongoing AI research, infrastructure, and new workplace accessibility solutions. Rylo intends to refine its AI models, broaden its toolset, and pursue a global expansion strategy to reach more individual users and enterprise customers.

1.1 ACTIVITIES
General Catalyst, established in 2000 and headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts, operates as a leading global investment and transformation firm dedicated to partnering with visionary entrepreneurs. Its core business activities revolve around providing venture capital across all stages-from seed funding to growth equity-while offering strategic advisory services, operational expertise, and ecosystem integration to cultivate resilient enterprises. The firm invests in a broad spectrum of sectors, including healthcare, financial technology, defense, industrials, climate, and energy, with a strong emphasis on fostering innovation that yields both financial returns and societal benefits. Among its fastest-growing segments, applied artificial intelligence emerges prominently, fueled by a landmark $8 billion capital commitment in October 2024 to accelerate AI-centric startups and transformations. Managing over $30 billion in assets, General Catalyst''s portfolio includes transformative companies such as Airbnb and Stripe, underscoring its pivotal role in scaling high-impact ventures worldwide.
