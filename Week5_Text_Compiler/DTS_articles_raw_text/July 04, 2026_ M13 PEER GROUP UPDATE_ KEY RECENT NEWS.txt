M13 General Partner Latif Peracha announced on April 15, 2026, that the firm would co-lead OpenFX's Series A funding round. Peracha cited a shared vision of enhancing the trading experience for both retail and institutional investors as a primary driver of the investment. OpenFX operates a digital asset platform focused on improving efficiency and transparency in foreign exchange markets, addressing pain points such as high transaction fees and slow settlement times. According to Peracha, M13's expertise in scaling technology companies positions it as a key strategic resource for OpenFX as the company moves to expand its technological infrastructure and grow its market reach. The Series A capital is earmarked for new product features tailored to the evolving needs of OpenFX's user base, with M13's operational guidance expected to play a central role in user acquisition and retention efforts.
Separately, Creative Biolabs announced on April 30, 2026, significant upgrades to its lambda and M13 phage library platforms, targeting advancements in biotherapeutic research. The upgraded lambda phage genome libraries now support large DNA inserts of up to 25 kilobases with enhanced fidelity, coverage, and long-term stability. Technical improvements include acoustic DNA shearing for reproducible fragmentation, optimized size-selection workflows, and ultra-efficient in vitro packaging extracts achieving titers exceeding 10 pfu per gram of DNA. In parallel, Creative Biolabs' upgraded M13 phage display platform supports the construction of ultra-diverse libraries reaching up to 10 to the power of 10 unique variants, enabling high-affinity antibody and peptide identification. The platform incorporates multiple display formats, including pIII monovalent and pVIII multivalent configurations, along with Trimer Codon Technology designed to eliminate stop codons and ensure uniform amino acid distribution. A company spokesperson stated, "Phage-based technologies continue to shape the future of biotechnology. Our upgraded lambda and M13 platforms reflect our commitment to delivering advanced, customizable, and reliable solutions that help researchers achieve meaningful scientific breakthroughs."
Turning to M13's venture peer General Catalyst, the firm completed its role in a major take-private transaction involving Janus Henderson Group Ltd., which was finalized on June 30, 2026. Under the terms of the deal, Janus Henderson's public shares were converted into cash at $52.00 per share and subsequently delisted from the New York Stock Exchange. The transaction was executed alongside Trian Fund Management and Qatar Investment Authority. Janus Henderson will continue to be led by Chief Executive Officer Ali Dibadj, with operational bases maintained in London and Denver. Dibadj characterized the privatization as the beginning of a transformative chapter, pointing to new opportunities in investment solutions, technology development, and talent acquisition. General Catalyst CEO Hemant Taneja commented on the collaboration, noting the firm's commitment to enhancing Janus Henderson's technological capabilities and overall growth strategy. The investor group supporting the transaction included global institutional players such as MassMutual and Sun Hung Kai & Co.
General Catalyst also participated in a $1.5 million seed funding round for Gurugram-based startup Omli, with the announcement made on June 17, 2026. The round was led by India Quotient. Founded in 2025 by Shivam Munshi and Siddhant Garg, Omli develops AI infrastructure specifically designed for children's speech and language development — an area largely underserved by existing commercial speech recognition systems, which are predominantly trained on adult voices. The company's flagship application, OmliKids, allows children aged three to ten to engage in voice-based conversations with an AI companion called Doro and has surpassed 500,000 downloads on the Google Play Store since launch. Omli has also developed a proprietary speech-to-text system called VedAnsh-v1, which has demonstrated meaningful reductions in word error rates when processing children's speech compared to commercial alternatives. Co-founder Siddhant Garg stated the company's goal is to achieve adult-grade accuracy in children's speech recognition, including support for Indian accents and vernacular languages. India Quotient's Madhukar noted that children represent the largest consumer segment for which AI-driven solutions are yet to be fully developed.
In a third significant development, General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund led an $85 million growth round for Rylo, an AI-powered communication platform formerly known as Nagish, with co-investors including Canaan, Vertex Ventures, and Contour. The round brings Rylo's total funding beyond $100 million. Headquartered across Tel Aviv and New York, Rylo provides accessibility-focused communication tools for Deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals, including real-time call captioning, speech-to-text conversion, text-to-speech capabilities, and in-person transcription services. Following its acquisition of Sign.mt in 2025, Rylo launched Rylo Sign, enabling real-time translation between spoken and sign languages. The platform also integrates speaker recognition, sentiment analysis, and fraud detection features. The newly raised capital will fund expansion of Rylo's engineering, product development, and commercial teams, alongside continued investment in AI research and workplace accessibility solutions.
Among M13's peer group, Lightspeed Venture Partners reported the sale of approximately $15.1 million in Netskope Inc. (NASDAQ: NTSK) Class A common stock across transactions dated June 12 and June 15, 2026. On June 12, 2026, Lightspeed Opportunity Fund, L.P. converted 1,650,000 Class B shares into Class A shares and subsequently sold 1,313,827 shares at a weighted average price of $9.19 per share. On June 15, 2026, the fund sold a further 336,173 Class A shares at a weighted average price of $9.00 per share, bringing total proceeds to $15,099,627. Following these transactions, Lightspeed Opportunity Fund, L.P. holds no remaining Class A common stock in Netskope, though other affiliated Lightspeed entities collectively held 62,843,207 Class B shares as of June 12, 2026, convertible into Class A shares on a one-for-one basis. Netskope shares traded at approximately $8.95 at the time of the sales, down roughly 60% over the preceding twelve months. On the financial performance front, Netskope reported annual recurring revenue of $845 million, a 29% year-over-year increase, alongside total revenue of approximately $202 million, representing 28% year-over-year growth. Analyst reactions were mixed, with TD Cowen reducing its price target from $25 to $19 while maintaining a Buy rating, citing the ARR miss and the departure of the company's CFO. BMO Capital and RBC Capital both lowered their targets to $13 with Outperform ratings, while Piper Sandler decreased its target to $18, acknowledging strong new logo ARR growth but flagging challenges with sales force productivity. Mizuho also trimmed its target to $13.
Lightspeed Venture Partners also moved to strengthen its marketing leadership with the appointment of Rohit Kaul as the firm's new marketing head. The hire follows the departure of former VP of Marketing and Communications Mansi Chugh and comes as Lightspeed prepares to raise its fifth India and Southeast Asia-focused fund, reportedly targeting approximately $500 million. Kaul previously served as Vice President of Marketing at Blume Ventures and has held roles at Sacra and HCL. Lightspeed has established a notable presence in India's startup ecosystem through investments in companies including OYO, Udaan, ShareChat, PhysicsWallah, and Pocket FM. The appointment is seen as part of a broader effort to reinforce the firm's brand presence and outreach capacity across the Indian and Southeast Asian markets amid a period of slower startup funding activity.

The startup has developed a platform aimed at streamlining and improving agent performance assessment processes. Lightspeed's back-to-back participation signals sustained conviction in the startup's growth trajectory. The capital is intended to support expansion of the company's product offerings, technological capabilities, and marketing initiatives as it works to establish a competitive presence in the agent evaluation space.
